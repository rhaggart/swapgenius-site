Software for running bike and ski gear swaps

Swap Genius helps clubs, teams and shops run a consignment gear swap from start to finish: seller sign-up, barcode tags, check-in, sales at the register, pickup and seller payouts.

Contact us

What Swap Genius does

Your own swap site

Each organizer gets a site at yourname.swapgenius.com with your logo, swap details and seller sign-in.

Seller accounts and tags

Sellers list their gear online and print barcode tags at home or at the swap. One account works at any swap that uses Swap Genius.

Fast check-in

Staff scan each tag to check gear in and print an inventory list for the seller to sign.

Point of sale

Scan tags at the register and take cash or card. Sales tax and your commission are calculated for you.

Pickup and payouts

Print a pickup list for each seller showing what sold, what is going home and what they are owed, then print payout checks.

Reports

See items and dollar totals, sales, commission and sales tax collected for each swap.

How it works

  1. Set up your swapChoose your dates, sales tax rate and commission, and connect your own Stripe account for card payments.
  2. Sellers sign upSellers create an account, enter their gear and prices, and get a barcode to show at check-in.
  3. Check in gearOn drop-off day, staff scan each tagged item into the swap.
  4. SellOn swap day, staff scan items at the register and take payment by cash or card.
  5. Pickup and pay sellersSellers collect unsold gear, and you pay each seller their share after the swap closes.

Pricing

Swap Genius is priced as a flat fee per swap, paid by the organizer. There is no charge to sellers or buyers for using the software.

Policies

Payments

Card payments made at a swap are processed by Stripe through the swap organizer's own Stripe account. Swap Genius does not store card numbers. Organizers pay Swap Genius fees by card through Stripe.

Refunds and cancellations

Organizers: if you cancel before seller sign-up opens for your swap, we refund the swap fee in full. After seller sign-up has opened, the fee is not refundable. To cancel, email rob@swapgenius.com.

Buyers and sellers: items bought at a swap are sold by the swap organizer, not by Swap Genius. Returns, refunds and seller payouts for those sales are handled by the organizer under the organizer's own policies.

Terms of service

Swap Genius provides software that organizers use to run their swaps. Organizers are responsible for their events, the gear sold at them, collecting and remitting sales tax, and paying their sellers. Sellers are responsible for the accuracy of their listings and for the gear they consign. We may suspend an account that is used unlawfully or that puts other users at risk. The service is provided as is, and our liability is limited to the fees paid to us for the swap in question.

Privacy

We collect the information needed to run a swap: organizer account details, and seller names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and item listings. A seller's contact details are shared only with the organizers of swaps that seller registers for. We do not sell personal information. We use service providers to host the software, send email and process payments. To see, correct or delete your information, email rob@swapgenius.com.
